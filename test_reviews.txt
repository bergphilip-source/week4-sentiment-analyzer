TLDR: Yeah... theyre the actual, very best wireless ANC phones. Sound is really high rez, and bright, Some say too bassy, but thats audio-snob jive. Fairly comfy, but you might want aftermarket pads if youre tender. ...and noone'd blame you if you wanna wait for em to go on sale. $$$. I did.
SOUND:
The drivers Sony put in this newest model are... elegant, silky.. They prettymuch do it all. You can hear literal words and background sylables and instruments you cant with sub-hundred-buck units. Those reverbs go on for ages, fading into the inky blackness very well because of the currently-best ANC. The highs are glassy, clear and crispy while bass is simultaneously rubbery and tight. Soundstage is very solid: Instruments are layered in and seperate sounding like sandwich toppings and not mixed and munddied together like an overstirred soup.
TUNING:
Dont let purists and audio-snobs online put you off with graphs and spreadsheets, specs or immaterial statements of their pholosophy. They have a real crowdpleaser sound. Yes, they have a V shape on sound graphs, however this IS NOT offensive to the ear at all. Theres a perfectly valid reason some manufacturers do that on purpose. The extra bass and treble are enough to appeal to younger folks, but tasteful and balanced enough for older or more traditional genre enthusiasts. Its got range. Not compromised range. I mean it does GREAT in a wide variety of applications. Talkshows, movies... country, metal, you cant miss. Nothing comes off wonky, screechy, or is missing.
A side note: i theorize this extra mid bass is tuned that way to assist the ANC do its job better. I had some cheap ANC Soundcore brand phones that had that extra mid bass. They had unusually powerful ANC for the price.
ANC:
literally the worlds best as of release. Its like body armor for your ears. Youll only dead black silence, tinnitus, alien telapathy and crazy whispering voices spewing lies about Airpods Max being better. (seriously, you gotta get those looked at). But yeah
sony paid good money to validify that statement for marketing. Specifically there is no "cabin pressure" sensation some units hiss at you with. True, low profile deadness. I live on a state highway and this cuts everything down to the faintest 'whoosh' but the occasional ricer fart-can exhaust or an ambulance... and even those are FAR more ignorable. I dont fly planes, but im confident this will squash most if not all of that endless rumble. Also there is no wierd, tacky sound effects when you switch it on and off, like some chinese-ium models tend to do.
PASSTHRU/AMBIENT MODE:
i can from personal experience report this, when it wants to be, is superior to most human hearing. Its extremely sensitive, borderline naturalistic sounding and uncompromizing. The phones can play at 25% volume or so while i keep an ear out for my crazy uncle as he curses at the TV in the den, from my bedroom. Perfectly servicable to hear a cashier or make brief small talk with some rando. I will say, the voice trigger on the auto-ambient mode is sensetive almost AGRESSIVE even. You so much as casually clear your nose or throat and like clockwork or make a faint comment to yourself and its ten seconds of passthrough mode for you. Regardless what the phone is doing at that time. Just be advised.
FIT/COMFORT:
Juuust slightly on the firm side. Not uncomfortable really, no. Youtubers sometimes say this will fade, so we shall see. Be advised these have a really protruding ANC device in each ear cup. (PHOTO). Some people with high profile ear anatomy may consider some aftermarket ear pads to compensate. Anyway i have accidentally slept in them and can attest prettymuch no discomfort. Some have mentioned the headband as being a bit subpar, but so far so good. The units are lightweight anyhow and therefore dont bear down on the head.
APP:
Its relatatively enormous. Like one of those goofy multitools wuth a hundred gizmos that wont fit in your pocket. By far the most all-inclusive audio app i have seen personally. 10 band EQ, Spatial audio calibration, all your usual toggles like Listening Mode. Codec toggling, it even seems to be able to upscale the seeming resolution on low rez mp3s. " strange red button, just says "Release the Hounds". too scared to press.
BATTERY:
I've not tried draining this units power, but from full it certainly trickles down slowly. The auto-shutoff and power saving functions are VERY agressive and 5 or 10 minutes off my head sees them deactivate. Uncertain wether this can even be disabled as of yet. I venture to bet that 30 hour claim is actually acurate. I would have heard more online reviewers complain...
GRIPES:
1: Bit bland looks.
The looks wont set the world on fire. I dare say slightly boring asthetics... but thats japan for you. Three very traditional colors... So I got the "platinum silver" folks these are a boring matte grey. The design is really minimalist.. but.. I can see it this way: Do you really want a loud colorful advertisement youre balancing $400 loosely on your exposed head in a busy subway or sidewalk? This is quiet luxury. I can cope.
2: stain MAGNET. Real talk if this thing collects any more body oil, America is liable to try to invade it. ..but for real though, I have dry skin and even I'm leaving a trail after a week.
3: Not Ambidexterous. I cant seem to find any way to swap the touch controls. Its right handed only? You should be able to use your dominant hand while you use a phone or mouse or pen, and the off hand for the touxh controls. It really clashes with your muscle memory.

Do not buy these.
Pros: They block out noise on the other side of your calls VERY well and the overall sound quality is decent until they stop working properly.
Cons: The longevity does not match the price point and neither does the customer service from Sony. I purchased these headphones 4/23/26 for $428 plus tax. I primarily use them for listening to music/streaming in the background while I work, and making phone calls. They do not leave my desk unless they are on my head. I do not wear them working out/ in any situation where they would be exposed to moisture of any kind. On 7/31/26 the right side started making a high pitched whining noise, and then a static/crackle. I did all of the online troubleshooting and it did go away. Today 9/1/26 the static/crackle came back and after another round of the troubleshooting it was much better but not 100%. I had them off for a couple of hours, now the right side is noticeably quieter with zero noise cancellation. If I hold the right side for ambient sound, it only comes through on the left.
Headphones this new, especially at this price point, should not be malfunctioning. I have spoken to customer service and am getting a new pair.. HOWEVER. To get your defective item, which is under warranty, replaced you have to send them back first. Which is annoying but mostly understandable. The timeline/process is not. They email you a shipping label. You need to write your case number and return # on the box. You must include a note with your name, case number, and phone number. The items need to be bubble wrapped and the box being filled at least 2-3 inches of packing peanuts or paper, and the item cannot move around. Then you drop them at FedEx. The shipping to them takes 7-10 business days. Once they receive the item it takes the warehouse up to 3 business days to acknowledge receipt. Then at some point they send you new ones, which have an additional 7-10 business day timeline. In my case, this puts me receiving my replacement in around 3 weeks. If you ask for faster shipping, they will say no and repeatedly insist you should be grateful they are going to replace their defective product without charging you to do so. If you are lucky, you might get a supervisor that is equally confused as to why they cannot give you an expedited shipping label. I did. I will not be purchasing another Sony product, ever, and neither should you.
